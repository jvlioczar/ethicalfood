Glossary

The language of alternative proteins

61 essential terms, in English and Portuguese, written for this site. For the full technical reference, see the GFI Brazil Cultivated Meat Glossary.

Sector

Alternative proteinProteína alternativa
Protein made from plants, by fermentation or by growing cells, rather than from raised animals.
Cellular agricultureAgricultura celular
Umbrella term for using cells and related technologies to make ingredients, food and other farm products. Not a synonym for cell cultivation.
Cultivated meatCarne cultivada
Meat made by growing animal cells directly. The term preferred by the sector.
Factory farmingPecuária industrial
Intensive animal production at industrial scale.
Food manufacturing facilityInstalação de produção de alimentos
The regulated plant where final products are made. The sector prefers this to "lab".
Hybrid productProduto híbrido
A food combining sources, such as cultivated cells with plant protein.
Life cycle assessment (LCA)Avaliação do ciclo de vida (ACV)
Method for estimating a product’s environmental impact from inputs to disposal.
Plant-basedÀ base de plantas
Made from plants and formulated to replace an animal product.

Technologies

3D bioprintingBioimpressão 3D
Depositing cells and materials in layers to build a tissue structure.
Biomass fermentationFermentação de biomassa
Growing fast-growing microorganisms, such as fungi, for their protein-rich mass, which is the food itself.
BioprocessBioprocesso
The sequence of steps that uses living cells to make a product at scale.
Cell cultivationCultivo celular
Taking cells from an animal or plant and giving them the nutrients and conditions to multiply and mature into tissue.
ExtrusionExtrusão
Pressing and heating plant protein to give it a fibrous, meat-like texture.
Gas fermentationFermentação gasosa
Fermentation in which microorganisms are fed gases such as CO₂ along with nutrients.
Molecular farmingAgricultura molecular
Engineering plants to produce a target ingredient, which is then extracted and purified.
Precision fermentationFermentação de precisão
Using microorganisms such as yeast to produce a specific functional ingredient, often by inserting the gene for it. Long used for rennet and insulin.
Scale-upEscalonamento
Moving a process from small volumes to industrial production.
Tissue engineeringEngenharia de tecidos
Combining cells, scaffolds and signals to build functional tissue.
Traditional fermentationFermentação tradicional
Using microbes to change the flavor or texture of ingredients, as in tempeh.

Cells and organisms

AdipocyteAdipócito
Fat cell, important to the flavor and texture of meat.
BiopsyBiópsia
Removing a small tissue sample, a common way of obtaining starter cells.
Cell bankBanco de células
A stored, characterized stock of cells used to start production.
Cell lineLinhagem celular
A population of cells that can be kept growing in culture.
DifferentiationDiferenciação
The process in which a cell becomes a specialized type, such as muscle.
FibroblastFibroblasto
Cell that makes connective tissue and the extracellular matrix.
Host strainCepa hospedeira
The microorganism engineered to make the target molecule in precision fermentation.
ImmortalizationImortalização
Changing cells so that they keep dividing beyond their normal limit.
MyceliumMicélio
The root-like network of threads of a fungus.
MycoproteinMicoproteína
Protein-rich food made from fungal biomass.
MyoblastMioblasto
Precursor cell that fuses with others to form muscle fibers.
Pluripotent stem cellCélula-tronco pluripotente
Stem cell able to become almost any type of cell in the body.
ProliferationProliferação
The phase in which cells multiply in number.
Satellite cellCélula satélite
Muscle stem cell, widely used as a starting point for cultivated meat.
SenescenceSenescência
The point at which cells permanently stop dividing.
Stem cellCélula-tronco
Unspecialized cell that can renew itself and turn into other cell types.

Culture media

Amino acidAminoácido
Building block of proteins and a key ingredient of culture media.
Culture mediumMeio de cultura
The nutrient liquid in which cells grow, usually the largest cost in cultivated meat.
FeedstockMatéria-prima
Raw material that feeds a fermentation or culture, such as sugars.
Fetal bovine serum (FBS)Soro fetal bovino (SFB)
Serum from calf fetuses, historically used in cell culture. The sector is working to replace it.
Growth factorFator de crescimento
Signaling protein that tells cells to grow or differentiate.
HydrolysateHidrolisado
Protein broken into smaller pieces, used as a low-cost nutrient source.
Serum-free mediumMeio sem soro
Culture medium made without animal serum.

Scaffolds

BiomaterialBiomaterial
Material designed to interact with living cells, as in scaffolds.
DecellularizationDescelularização
Removing cells from a tissue, such as a plant, to reuse its structure.
Extracellular matrix (ECM)Matriz extracelular (MEC)
The network of proteins and sugars around cells in tissue.
HydrogelHidrogel
Water-rich polymer gel used to hold cells.
MicrocarrierMicrocarreador
Tiny bead that gives adherent cells a surface to grow on in a bioreactor.
ScaffoldSuporte (scaffold)
A structure that supports cells and gives tissue its shape.

Bioreactors and process

BioreactorBiorreator
The vessel that gives cells or microbes controlled conditions to grow.
ContaminationContaminação
Unwanted microbes entering a culture, a key operational risk.
Downstream processingProcessamento a jusante
The steps after growth that harvest and purify the product.
Fed-batchBatelada alimentada
Adding nutrients during a run without removing the culture.
PerfusionPerfusão
Continuously replacing medium while keeping cells in the reactor.
Shear stressTensão de cisalhamento
Force from moving fluid, which can damage cells.
Stirred-tank bioreactorBiorreator de tanque agitado
Common bioreactor design that mixes contents with an impeller.

Market and regulation

AnalogAnálogo
A product designed to replace a specific animal product.
B2B / B2CB2B / B2C
Selling to other businesses, or directly to consumers.
Contract manufacturerFabricante terceirizado
A company that makes products for other brands.
GRASGRAS
"Generally recognized as safe", a US food ingredient status.
Novel foodNovo alimento
A regulatory category for foods without a history of consumption, such as in the EU.
Price parityParidade de preço
When a new product costs the same as the conventional one.

How to talk about it

Based on the guide Key Terms in Cellular Agriculture (Cellular Agriculture Australia, 2023). The guide also advises against calling products "ethical" or "sustainable" in absolute terms, because this cannot be measured. On this site, "ethical" describes the question driving the project, not a label on each company.

PreferAvoidWhy
Cultivated meatLab-grown, in vitro, test-tube, vat-grownSounds unnatural and is inaccurate: final products are made in food plants.
Cultivated meatSynthetic meatToo broad, with negative connotations.
Cultivated meatSlaughter-free, cruelty-freeValue-laden and derogatory to traditional producers.
Cultivated meatFake meat, frankenmeat, schmeatDerogatory and not descriptive.
Cultivated meatClean meatValue-laden, and does not distinguish it from plant-based meat.
Cellular agricultureCell-based food productionToo narrow: not all products are food or animal-derived.
Precision-fermentedAcellularNot widely understood.
Functionally equivalentNature-identical, dairy-identical"Identical" is not technically accurate and may carry legal risk.
